# Wyoming v1 — final report (2026-10-27)

**80/149 catalog sections exact, 29 approximate, 40 pending; 6/24 rivers audited verified; 80/149 ready for visitors; 17 more sections are drafts.**

## What was done
- WGFD's statewide River Access Points layer (185 launch/egress points) plus one NPS point (Southgate, Snake River) were cross-matched against WGFD Facilities and Public Access Areas, BLM recreation sites, USFS recreation opportunities and NPS POIs.
- Every point got an NHD High Resolution 150 m check (local HU4 GeoPackages because the NHDPlus HR service returned 504s) and an Esri imagery tile; published pins were reviewed visually.
- Sections were built from WGFD's own launch→egress order (USGS NLDI chainage); miles are preliminary NHDPlus V2 routed distance except six NPS Grand Teton/Flagg sections that use NPS mileage.
- Restrictions (12) attached per section: WY-RES-001 permit; WY-RES-002 closure; WY-RES-003 inspection; WY-RES-004 access-law; WY-RES-005 permit-fee; WY-RES-006 permit; WY-RES-007 tribal-permit; WY-RES-008 tribal-approval; WY-RES-009 seasonal-closure; WY-RES-010 seasonal-access; WY-RES-011 hazard; WY-RES-012 hazard.
