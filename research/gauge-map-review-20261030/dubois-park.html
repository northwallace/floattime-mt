Parks & Recreation

The Town of Dubois has approximately 13 acres of deeded property that is used only as a recreational area. Bordering the Wind River, a walking path meanders through native brush where wildlife is in abundance. Nearby, a playground with picnic areas are available and a covered shelter area can be reserved. Public restrooms are available Spring through mid-Fall. Please click on the Facilities tab for more information and reservation information

For park reservations click on the Facilities tab and reserve your space or contact the Clerk's Office at (307) 455-2345
