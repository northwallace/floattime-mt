1.2 Study Area 
 
 
Figure 1.  Location of the Middle Methow Reach of the Methow River. 
 
This recreational use assessment focuses on the Methow River between the towns of 
Winthrop and Twisp, Wash., in Okanogan County (see Figure 1).  This area extends 
from approximately River Mile 41 to River Mile 50 and is known as the Middle 
Methow Reach of the Methow River.
