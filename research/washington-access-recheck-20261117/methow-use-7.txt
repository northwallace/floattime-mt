Generally, the number of daily recreational users interacting with the river in the 
Middle Methow Reach increases with the ambient air temperature and with the 
decrease in the river’s flow.  For this reason, the highest amounts and highest variety 
of river recreation use in the Middle Methow Reach occur in July and August.
