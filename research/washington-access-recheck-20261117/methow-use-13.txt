Aero Methow is the local medical and rescue service that would respond to river 
rescue situations on the Middle Methow Reach of the Methow River.  Aero Methow 
is located in the Town of Twisp and is the only swiftwater response team in the area.  
The 10-member team also responds to low-angle and high-angle rescues.   
 
Aero Methow possesses all of the necessary equipment and training to execute most 
river responses.  In addition to quarterly water training, the Aero Methow team 
undergoes on-going training and is re-certified in swiftwater response and rescue 
every three years. 
 
Aero Methow has requested that a detailed map of the M2 site locations be 
provided. The information to be included will be the address, river right or left 
location, road mile markers and a complete description of each site.
