Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Float ETA (“we,” “us,” or “this site”) at floateta.com is a river float-planning website. This policy explains what information is collected and which other services the site calls. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not ask for payment information.

  • River reports you submit. If you post an obstruction report, we store the river, hazard type, optional location, description, optional name, and the time it was submitted. Vote counts (“still there” / “cleared”) are stored with the report. Do not include phone numbers, addresses, or other personal details in a report.
  • Repeated-vote checks. We store a hash derived from the connection address and report ID to limit repeated votes on a report. This internal value is not included in the public reports feed. People on a shared network may be treated as the same voter.
  • Server logs. Our host (currently Render) may log standard request data such as IP address, browser type, date/time, and the page requested. We use this to run and debug the site, not to build marketing profiles.
  • Optional put-in time. Launch time is used in your browser to estimate take-out time. Your selected river, craft, fishing preference, and optional launch time are included in the page address so you can share your plan. Opening or reloading that address sends these selections to the website and they may appear in standard server logs. Shared launch times refer to today when opened.
  • Optional map location. If you tap the location control on a verified access map and grant browser permission, your browser uses your device location to place the blue marker. Float ETA does not save that coordinate. The map provider receives the map area needed to return imagery and labels, which can reveal the general area you are viewing.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact page, we store your name, email, message, and the time it was sent so we can read it and reply.
  • Corrections and float requests. We store the river and section or requested route, your suggested change, any supporting link, and the submission time in a private review queue. Your name and email are optional and are not published. Suggestions are reviewed before river information changes.

Information we do not collect on purpose

We do not sell personal information and we do not run a user login. Names and emails are collected if you send them through the contact form or optionally include them with a correction or float request, plus the optional name on a public river report.

Third-party services

The site loads data from other companies. Those services have their own privacy policies:

  • USGS Water Data API — live streamflow and water temperature. Float ETA’s server requests and briefly caches this public data, so your browser does not contact USGS directly. See the USGS privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo — air temperature, wind, rain chance, and UV at the put-in. Your browser requests this data directly. See Open-Meteo terms.
  • Esri and UNPKG — satellite imagery, road labels, and the MapLibre library files used on verified access maps. Your browser requests these files directly. See the Esri privacy statement and Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Render — hosts the website. See Render’s privacy policy.

Advertising

Float ETA does not currently display ads and does not load an advertising network. If that changes, this policy will be updated before ads go live.

How we use information

  • To show live river conditions and float-time estimates
  • To publish and moderate community obstruction reports
  • To keep the site working, secure, and backed up

How long we keep reports

Obstruction reports are hidden after 45 days unless someone confirms they are still there. Clear votes are advisory and do not automatically hide reports. Hidden reports may remain in our stored data for a time for abuse review, then are discarded in the ordinary course of operating the site.

Children

This site is about outdoor recreation and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • You can use the site without submitting a report or a name.
  • Before sharing a plan, you can clear the launch-time field if you do not want to include that time.
  • You can block third-party requests in your browser. Weather is requested directly from Open-Meteo, so blocking that request hides weather details. Live flow comes through Float ETA’s server-side USGS proxy.
  • You can request that we delete a report that contains your personal information by contacting us with enough detail to find it (river, date, and the text of the report).

Security

No website is perfectly secure. We take reasonable steps to protect report data, but you should not submit secrets, exact home addresses, or other sensitive personal information in a river report.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site or hosting changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact form. Include a real email if you want a reply.
