How the math works

1

Live gauge data

Every river here is tied to a USGS streamgage. We pull the instantaneous discharge (cfs) and water temperature, refreshed every time you load the page.

2

Flow → speed

River speed rises with discharge. We calibrate each river's typical drift speed at a reference flow, then scale it using the hydraulic-geometry relationship v ∝ Q0.34.

3

Your craft matters

A paddled kayak outruns the current; a tube with a cooler lags behind it. We adjust the estimate for what you're actually floating in.

4

Beat the sunset

We compute sunset at the put-in and tell you the latest reasonable launch time, so you're not navigating strainers in the dark.
