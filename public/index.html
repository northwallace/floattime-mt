How the math works

1

Live gauge data

We use reviewed USGS gauges and selected Reclamation and Colorado DWR discharge stations. The source and reading age appear beside the flow. USGS readings are cached for five minutes; Reclamation and Colorado DWR readings for fifteen. Water temperature is shown when available. Estimates require a calibrated river model and a positive reading from the last two hours.

2

Flow → speed

River speed rises with discharge. Where a reference-flow model is available, we scale its reference drift speed using the hydraulic-geometry relationship v ∝ Q0.34.

3

Your craft matters

A paddled kayak outruns the current; a tube with a cooler lags behind it. We adjust the estimate for what you're actually floating in.

4

Beat the sunset

We compute sunset at the put-in and tell you the latest reasonable launch time, so you're not navigating strainers in the dark.

    Float-time questions

    Short answers to what people ask before they put in.

    How long does it take to float a river?

    It depends on today's flow, the stretch, and your craft. A 9-mile Class I run might be 2.5 hours in a raft at a healthy cfs and closer to 4 hours on a tube or if you stop to fish. Float ETA offers live estimates where a reference-flow model is established. Other stretches show published guide times when available, alongside access and flow information.

    How does Float ETA calculate float time?

    Each river names its flow source. Where a timing model is available, we take a reference raft drift speed at a reference flow, then scale it with hydraulic geometry (speed rises with discharge to the 0.34 power). Kayaks, tubes, and paddleboards get their own multipliers. Optional fishing adds 25 minutes per mile.

    What CFS is good for floating?

    There is no single safe flow. The badge compares the gauge with a reference flow; it cannot establish whether a stretch is suitable. Check access, weather, hazards, and local guidance.

    How much extra time if I stop to fish?

    Turn on Stopping to fish and we add 25 minutes per mile on top of drift time. A 9-mile stretch gains about 3 hours 45 minutes.

    Which rivers does Float ETA cover?

    Browse the river directory for current coverage and access points. New rivers are added regularly.

    Can I still launch if it is getting late?

    The calculator uses sunset at the put-in and tells you the latest reasonable launch, or your take-out time if you enter a put-in. Leave a buffer — strainers after dark are a bad plan.
